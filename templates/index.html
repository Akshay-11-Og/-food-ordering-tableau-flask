Overview

The Story Behind the Data

A capstone project analysing food orders to understand which cuisines customers prefer and how demand changes over time.

The Question

Which cuisines are in highest demand, and how do order volumes and revenue shift across time, location and customer segments?

The Data

Order-level food delivery data covering cuisines, order dates, order values and customer details, prepared and modelled in Tableau.

The Approach

Interactive dashboards with filters let you slice the data yourself and discover patterns instead of reading static charts.
